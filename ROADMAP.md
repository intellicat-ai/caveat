# Roadmap

Planned work, in no fixed order. Nothing listed here exists yet.

## Mappings

- **MeSH alignment.** SKOS mappings from CAVEAT unreliability modes and
  detection markers to Medical Subject Headings descriptors, with every
  descriptor ID checked against the NLM MeSH service. Confirm that the MeSH
  terms of use permit redistribution of the identifiers and labels used.
- **Retraction Watch reason alignment.** Mappings from the retraction
  reasons used in the Retraction Watch database to CAVEAT unreliability
  modes, for use with stated-reason evidence links. Confirm the licence of
  the reason vocabulary before publishing it.

## Ontology

- New unreliability modes and detection markers, including classes with
  more than one parent.
- Expanded controlled vocabularies.
